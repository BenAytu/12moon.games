TV Mirror: Screen Mirroring

Mirror your phone, tablet or computer to your TV. Smoothly, over your home Wi-Fi, with nothing to install on the device you mirror from.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

TV Mirror is an app you install on your TV, not on your phone. Once it is open, your TV shows up in the screen mirroring menus that are already built into your devices:

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: the TV appears in Screen Mirroring, the same menu you would use for an Apple TV.
  • Windows PC: open the address shown on the TV in Chrome or Edge and press Share screen. There is no program to download.
  • Android phones and tablets: use the Cast or Screen Cast button in Quick Settings. This uses the Google Cast receiver built into Android TV and Google TV, and TV Mirror shows you how to find it.

Built for smooth playback

Screen mirroring over Wi-Fi has to deal with a network that is never perfectly steady. Many mirroring apps try to show every frame the instant it arrives, which looks fine for a second and then stutters whenever Wi-Fi hiccups. TV Mirror deliberately keeps a small, steady buffer, usually well under a second, and plays frames at an even pace. The result is a picture that stays smooth during videos, presentations and games, with sound kept in sync with the image.

Requirements

  • An Android TV or Google TV running Android 8.0 or newer.
  • The TV and the device you mirror from on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • TV Mirror open on the TV while you mirror. It does not run in the background, so when you close it your TV disappears from the lists.
  • For Windows: an up-to-date Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Privacy by design

Your screen and sound travel directly from your device to your TV inside your home network. They are never sent to us or to any server, recorded or stored. TV Mirror has no accounts and no cloud service. Details are in the TV Mirror privacy policy and the terms of service.
