About 12 Moon Games

We are a small independent studio from Istanbul, Türkiye, founded by developer Aytuğ Bengü.

12 Moon Games started with small mobile games: quick to learn, fun to share and built by a tiny team that cares about the details. Over time we became just as interested in the screens around the house, which led to TV Mirror, our app that turns an Android TV or Google TV into a screen mirroring receiver for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android.

What we believe in

  • Do one thing well. Each app has a clear purpose and no clutter.
  • Smooth beats flashy. We tune for steady performance on everyday hardware and Wi-Fi.
  • Respect for your privacy. We collect as little as possible and say exactly what we do.
  • Listen to players. Feedback from our users shapes every update.

Where to find us

Our apps are published on Google Play under the developer name 12 Moon Games. For anything else, see the contact page.
