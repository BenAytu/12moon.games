Horror Mall

You are trapped inside an abandoned shopping mall. The only way out is a car in the parking lot, but its tank is empty.

In development for Android

The goal

Search the dark shops, corridors and service rooms for twelve fuel cans. Carry each one back, fill the car and get out before the night is over. Sounds simple, until you hear something moving in the next aisle.

Survive the creatures

Mysterious creatures roam the halls and they will do anything to stop you. Stay out of sight, listen carefully and pick your route. Running makes noise; hiding takes time. Every can you find makes the mall a little more dangerous.

Better together

Play alone or team up with a friend in co-op. Split up to search faster, or stick together and watch each other's backs.

Features

  • Dark, tense atmosphere built for headphones
  • Twelve fuel cans hidden across the mall: find them all to escape
  • Simple, intuitive touch controls
  • Smooth performance tuned for phones
