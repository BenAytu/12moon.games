Toilet Survivor

An action shooter set somewhere no hero ever wanted to be. Hold the line, wave after wave.

In development for Android

How it plays

Enemies keep coming and there is very little room to move. Toilet Survivor is about positioning: find the best corner, watch your angles and make every shot count. Each wave is a little faster and a little meaner than the last.

Two players

Team up with a friend and split the work: one holds the door while the other covers the flank. Coordination beats firepower.

Features

  • Fast, wave-based action in tight spaces
  • Two-player mode
  • Strategy and reflexes in equal measure
  • Short rounds that are perfect for a quick break
