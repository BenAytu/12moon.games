TV Mirror: Screen Mirroring

TV Mirror turns an Android TV or Google TV into a screen mirroring receiver. Show your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows PC or Android phone on the big screen over your home Wi-Fi, without installing anything on the device you are mirroring from. It is built for smooth, stutter-free video and clear stereo sound.

Coming soon to Google Play
